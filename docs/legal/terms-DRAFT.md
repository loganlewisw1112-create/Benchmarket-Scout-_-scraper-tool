## 1. What Benchmark Scout is

Benchmark Scout ("the service") builds competitor reports for local businesses
from public information: OpenStreetMap data and the public websites of the
businesses involved. It is operated by [legal name / entity] ("we").

## 2. Open beta

The service is a free beta. It may change, break, or be unavailable, and we
may limit or end it at any time. Reports can be incomplete when public sources
are sparse or unreachable; missing information is shown as "N/A" rather than
estimated.

## 3. What reports are — and are not

- Reports describe **public signals** (for example, whether a homepage shows a
  phone number or testimonials). They are directional observations, not
  verified facts about any company's internal operations, finances, or quality.
- Reports are **not** professional, legal, financial, or marketing advice.
  Decisions you make based on a report are your own.
- Scores and ranks compare only what could be observed at the time of the
  report. Each report lists its sources and their dates.

## 4. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

- submit information you have no right to submit, or use the service to
  harass, defame, or target any person or business;
- attempt to overload, probe, or bypass the service's rate limits or security;
- present a report as an official rating, review, or endorsement of any
  business;
- scrape or resell the service or its reports in bulk without our written
  permission.

## 5. Shared reports

A report link can be opened by anyone who has it. Reports are kept for about
90 days. Don't share links you want to keep private.
